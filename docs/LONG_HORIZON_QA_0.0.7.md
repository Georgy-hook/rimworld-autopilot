# 0.0.7 long-horizon colony QA

The colony was replayed from a named checkpoint at 3× speed, with automatic 1× pacing during a verified home fire or critical bleeding. This note reports completed game outcomes, not API acknowledgements. Local saves and logs are intentionally excluded from the repository.

## Observed outcomes

- The first ~5.6 game days ended with the original three permanent colonists, no completed sale, and little research progress. Routine food decisions repeatedly displaced the chosen long-horizon course.
- By ~13 game days, one permanent colonist joined through a deserter quest. Two refugees were temporary workers; they later betrayed the colony and did not represent population growth.
- At ~18.8 days, the original controller allowed a fire after that betrayal to peak at 58 active flames and left three permanent colonists downed. Replaying the same checkpoint with a batch firefighter response held the fire to seven flames and extinguished it without a permanent casualty in that incident.
- At ~20 days in the fire replay, four permanent colonists remained; the colony had nine completed research projects, about 20.7k wealth, about three days of food, four medicine, and zero confirmed sales. The saved doctrine was `research_starflight` / `crops` / `ship_escape`. Laya had selected Pemmican to prepare a settlement trade route.
- A subsequent wolf attack exposed a second blocker. Two colonists died. One downed pawn had bleeding rate 11.76, but Laya ordered a 61-cell rescue to a bed instead of tending on the ground. Another bleeding pawn waited for that rescue order to complete. A pre-attack checkpoint and a failure save were retained locally for replay.
- In the later checkpoint replay, a hostile shambler with zero bleeding repeatedly recovered after being downed. Laya offered it prisoner recruitment and sale, then chose to leave it alive; the repeat attacks wounded a colonist. In the direct repair replay, Laya tended the bleeding colonist in the field, ordered a healthy fighter to finish the shambler, and kept that attack job running until the threat disappeared. All four colonists survived this encounter.
- A subsequent gazelle encounter downed two colonists. Both later received care; all four were still alive and Pemmican research resumed. A visiting shaman trader offered supplies, but the verified trade preview contained **zero sale options** for colony stock. Laya bought nothing. This was a real trade opportunity, yet produced no income.
- Around tick 2,239,000, another raid arrived while one colonist was still downed. That colonist died, two other defenders were downed, and the remaining defender was overwhelmed. Another colonist appeared in the roster, but that did not prevent the defensive collapse. The run was paused and saved at tick 2,244,066 with 13 active hostiles and three of four current colonists downed. The incident is an unresolved release blocker.
- A follow-up replay from the ~20-day checkpoint accepted a random incapacitated wanderer, raising permanent population to five. The newcomer remained downed and had to be fed, so this increased care demand before adding labor. Another attack injured multiple defenders, but all five remained alive immediately afterward. This does not establish repeatable recruitment.
- The follow-up also found a build API failure: the chosen room cell was valid for a campfire only at a nondefault rotation. The director now checks RimWorld's read-only `builder/site-options` result for the exact cell and uses the accepted rotation. In the live repeat, Laya chose the campfire again, RIMAPI accepted the blueprint, and the campfire was completed in that room.
- In that follow-up, a different visiting trader completed two sales: one piece of apparel for about 10.61 silver and 62 rice for about 45.09 silver. These are recorded executed transactions, not a stock valuation. The result proves that Laya can earn silver from a compatible visitor; regular income and a reliable buyer route remain unverified.
- Reloading the older game save with controller state from a later tick reset the saved doctrine, so the early follow-up interval was not a valid test of strategic continuity. The controller now retains a doctrine chosen before the restored game tick when at least one colonist identity still matches, while clearing future orders. For the running QA replay, the exact previously chosen doctrine and income route were restored from the earlier controller checkpoint; no game resources or pawn states were edited.
- With five permanent colonists, the follow-up passed the tick of the first replay's defensive collapse; the random 13-hostile raid did not recur. Research advanced only to Pemmican 165/500 by about tick 2,293,000, while raw food exceeded 200 and prepared meals remained in single digits. Laya eventually reserved a researcher again. Repeated early-harvest requests returned a stale-designation error despite ample raw crops; the director now suppresses this action when raw food is abundant and treats already designated plants as a harmless stale choice.
- At tick 2,408,511 the five-colonist follow-up was still alive, with two completed sales and Pemmican 248/500, but only six ready meals. Laya had reserved a researcher, then ordinary hunting and cooking priorities were assigned to that same pawn. The director now records the chosen researcher and prefers another eligible worker for routine jobs; a true staffing shortage can still override the reservation. This final staffing correction has unit coverage and is running in the continuing replay.

## Corrections under replay

The director now keeps strategic decisions available until food becomes a near-term shortage, reserves a researcher for the active project, redirects a fresh unrelated research selection, and offers a live Pemmican recipe when a caravan is needed. It checks prison materials and finished art before offering those actions, and filters quest choices with no executable follow-through.

During a home fire, it assigns every capable firefighter in one cycle and defers routine actions. During critical bleeding, it removes long rescues when field tending is possible, revisits other untreated patients without waiting for the first rescue, and lets a rescuer switch to tending their patient. The observer uses 1× during critical bleeding and when an active enemy is within 40 cells while a colonist is downed; distant threats leave normal 3× play intact. An isolated gunner facing a healthy melee attacker is offered an immediate backstep while distant allies cannot cover them. A replay from the pre-attack save passed the original wolf attack tick with all four permanent colonists alive, but the random wildlife encounter did not repeat. The shambler fix was verified in the live replay. After the later collapse, new hunting choices were gated while colonists recover and food is in store; a follow-up replay from the earlier checkpoint is underway.

## Remaining release gates

1. Survive a comparable later raid with recovered defenders and working triage. The first large raid defeated the colony; the replay passed the same tick, but that raid did not recur. The 1× emergency pacing was observed during a nearby hostile attack and returned to 3× afterward.
2. Turn the first two small sales into repeatable income. One trader could not buy any verified colony surplus; another bought rice and apparel for about 55.70 silver in total. A safe caravan route with travel food and home defenders remains unverified.
3. Demonstrate a repeatable permanent recruitment route. Incidental joins and temporary refugees do not establish one; head count also hides deaths.
4. Complete a real ship victory workflow. Current code can only plan a ship blueprint after the research chain. It has no verified reactor startup, casket loading, launch action, or victory confirmation; the `EndGame_ShipEscape` quest also lacks an automated route. Autonomous victory is therefore impossible with the present action surface.
5. Raise cooked-meal throughput and research progress with five colonists. The corrected campfire placement succeeded live, but food work still often displaced the saved ship plan. Observe the effect of the already accepted monument quest; newly offered unsupported monument quests are deferred.

Python suite: 359 tests pass, and the changed modules compile. The required RIMAPI C# build could not run on the test host because no .NET SDK is installed; no C# files were changed.
