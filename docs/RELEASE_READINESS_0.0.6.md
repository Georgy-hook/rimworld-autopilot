# 0.0.6 release readiness — 28 September 2026

Status: **final 0.0.6 scope with known verification gaps**. This records the evidence and limits of the release; it does not promise that an autonomous colony can never fail. The construction and fire changes first developed for 0.0.7 are included in 0.0.6. See [the focused audit](CONSTRUCTION_FIRE_AUDIT_0.0.6.md).

| Area | Evidence | Remaining verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early food | Test 13 clean replay: Laya collected mature berries, left all 80 young rice plants intact, then harvested ripe rice. Test 14 started as a separate fresh colony; all three founders were alive at the 4th Jugust checkpoint with 11 meals and 366 raw food items. | Continue Test 14 beyond the controlled repair/reload checkpoints, including later scarcity and raids. This does not yet establish durable autonomous survival. |

All three founders were alive at the checkpoint. | Continue watching the backlog and food safety across later days; confirm the latest compact action log in a running director. |
