## Scenario boundaries and absent telemetry

Disease: visible immunity/severity/treatment timing reach the comparison; no future immunity forecast or survival guarantee is inferred. Patients without available doctors/medicine/jobs produce no care option. Rescue: downed victims can justify limited cold/gas/fallout exposure, while the rescuer's bed and other job targets still undergo checks. Rescue remains distinct from treatment, feeding and observed recovery. Cold: thermostats are meaningful only for existing owned devices; building insulation, power and beds require architecture actions elsewhere. A setpoint does not prove safe room temperature.

Insects: thick mountain roof and hive observations expose infestation risk. Door choke requires actual walls, mobile armored melee and supporting ranged pawns; native positions still reject traps, fire, blocked cells and occupied cells. Kidnapping: carried IDs alone are insufficient; player ownership and kidnapping intent are required. Interception does not establish successful victim recovery. After combat, native rescue/tend options can become feasible again; the audit does not establish that every released prisoner/carried pawn returns to the colony or that autonomous jobs complete.

Missing shootable_opponent_ids keeps legacy range behavior and can overstate clear shots; current producer supplies explicit IDs. Missing can_fight remains legacy permissive Python behavior, with native violent-work validation on native options; generic positioning can still move civilians intentionally. Missing visibility defaults to visible in Python, while current producers exclude invisible hediffs. Unknown/malformed context cannot authorize a stale resilience or society POST.
