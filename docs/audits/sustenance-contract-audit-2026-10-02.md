# Sustenance, production, capability and ship contracts: offline audit

Audited 2026-10-02 against the installed Assembly-CSharp.dll and Core XML defs. No game, observer, director or model weights were launched. Tests use producer-shaped JSON and deterministic decision doubles; these prove contracts, not colony outcomes.

## Concrete fixes

* `SustenanceHelper.Context` produces fresh eligible food nutrition, perishables, cooler power/temperature/target and food-policy allowances. Python previously discarded these before native choice: `_stage` did not use its context, cooler effects had no matching row, and diet effects had no allowed stock. The stage now retains compact food and rot facts, marks missing nutrition unknown rather than zero, and projects actual cooler/diet facts into consequences. Server-provided risk is retained instead of replaced by a generic kind warning.
* Non-food recipe choices now export the loaded recipe's output definitions/counts and retain native risk in Python. This lets component/medicine/equipment benefits refer to actual products.
* `ProductionHelper` advertised kibble tables with a full 15-bill stack and did not check table-specific recipe availability. Its context now suppresses full tables without a reusable completed bill, and both observation and mutation check `recipe.AvailableOnNow(table)`.
* Sustenance's cleaning job response checked only `CurJobDef`, which could report success when another job of the same def remained current. It now checks the actual created job reference, consistent with its gather/fishing handlers.
* Rejected sustenance/production posts now enter their existing 15,000-tick action cooldown. This bounds repeated native rejection without claiming completed work. Production mutation responses require the Boolean `applied: true`; strings or malformed responses no longer count as success or crash recipe/logistics handling.
* Capability orders previously defaulted absent `applied` to true. Acceptance now follows actual producer contracts: `CapabilityOrderResultDto.applied`, created `GrowingZoneDto` with the selected plant and positive cells, `ResearchProjectDto.name`, or non-generic `ApiResult.success` for harvest/equip. Rejected harvests no longer change work priorities.
* Capability worker priority zero is intentionally retained: crop/training actions explicitly enable their chosen capable worker after acceptance; native animal handler checking uses `WorkTypeIsDisabled`, not current priority. A regression covers zero-priority grower plus real zone DTO. Incapable work types and protected care jobs remain excluded.
* The dedicated ship layout previously overlapped large native footprints, supplied one engine although native launch requires three, disconnected parts, and capped caskets at eight. `ship_blueprint` now uses native North-facing geometry, one cardinal connected component, three engines, free casket interaction cells and capacity for every current colonist.

## Route limitations and shared-owner findings

The ship fix is geometry only. The parent owner was notified that `build_ship` marked issued after the first blueprint call, while prepare required no computer core and no issued ship. Native casket placement needs a later stage after completed beams; the initial partial response must not close the construction route. Whole-layout research/material prerequisites, AI persona core, actual completed/connected launch parts, reactor startup and occupied caskets must be verified by the ending route.

Generic `catalog_construction_options` exposes unlocked affordable loaded definitions by category and compatible stuff, including ship/royal definitions. A catalog affordance alone does not choose an attached ship location or place royal furniture within the assigned throne/bedroom. `place_checked_building` validates native placement near an anchor, and completed plans are verified; contextual endgame placement needs route-level evidence. `plan_architecture` also previously recorded a project and returned applied after any blueprint response; this partial-result concern was reported to the parent owner.

Starting-food harvesting/hunting/shelter/wood/power and research prioritization are director/architecture responsibilities. These modules support usable food/butchery tables, crop choices, storage/cooling, pens/diets, and researched production; they do not prove a colony can survive every biome or complete every ending. Default RimWorld has no water-consumption subsystem in these APIs; water terrain/fishing is not a drinking resource.

No live path, reservation, sowing/harvest, food poisoning, raid, reactor startup, animal operation or launch was exercised. Native supply checks are estimates, production/hauling remains ordinary pawn work, and missing fields retain uncertainty instead of proving feasibility.
