# People, care and society coverage

Verified against installed RimWorld **1.6.4871 rev590**, Core + Royalty + Ideology + Biotech + Anomaly. Odyssey is not active. Installed XML and Assembly-CSharp are the authoritative implementation evidence; community pages provide supplementary player terminology, not executable eligibility rules.

## Implemented additions

`colony_society.py` owns `society_medical_care`, `society_prisoner_policy`, `society_free_time`. `SocietyController.cs`, `SocietyAutomationHelper.cs`, `SocietyDtos.cs` expose `/api/v1/society/context` and `/api/v1/society/policy`. The collector is read only. Director integration stores its return in `development.society`, calls `prepare`, registers descriptions/domains, and delegates choice/execution/assessment. Installation must include this Python module; the common C# build discovers the new controller.

Every policy uses live exported options, a native Laya person choice, a second Laya policy choice, and **defer at both stages**. Benefit, cost, risk and consequences of leaving the current policy are provided. Python recollects before posting; C# checks current target/map/control/eligibility again. Successful policy changes and explicit Laya deferrals enter the 15,000 tick cooldown; stale refusals do not. These are settings for normal work givers, not completed treatment, recruitment, conversion or learning. No pawn mood, relationship, skill, health or faction is edited directly.

* Medicine ceiling: live hediff severity, immunity, life threatening status, stock, care policy, enabled healthy doctor availability and actual Medicine skill are visible. `NoMeds` means doctor care without medicine; **NoCare is not offered**. Changing a ceiling neither reserves reachable medicine nor assigns an immediate tend job. Existing director urgent tend/rescue/feed actions remain responsible for emergency jobs.
* Prisoner policy: live resistance, current exclusive goal, ideology/certainty, available enabled wardens and Social skill. Recruit, maintain, resistance reduction and conversion are offered only when supported. Resistance reduction requires remaining resistance. Conversion requires active nonclassic Ideology and an available warden of a different ideology. Only adult prisoners are supported by this addition; wild people cannot receive ordinary recruit/resistance orders. Switching goals pauses the old goal; it does not reset resistance/certainty or touch nonexclusive prisoner interactions. Conversion follows the normal acting warden's ideology, not a guaranteed colony target ideology.
* Learning/recreation time: actual NeedDef instances and active child learning desires are exported; children need a Joy or Anything schedule to autonomously learn. A selected **single Work hour** becomes Joy. Sleep/Anything/other hours are preserved. Downed/drafted/mental state/active tend/rescue/feed/surgery pawns are excluded in both Python and C#. This allows activity but does not create a school, teacher, safe outdoor area or materials.

## Coverage matrix

| System | Observed context and existing support | New executable support | Honest gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Injury, bleeding, rescue, nutrition | Existing `colony_director.py`: `tend_colonist`, `rescue_downed_colonist`, `rescue_neutral_arrival`, patient feeding, doctor priorities and beds | Medicine policy alongside urgency actions | Diagnosis-specific surgery and routine operations beyond existing augmentation are not added |
| Infection, immunity, chronic disease | Actual hediff severity, life threatening flag and immunizable immunity, including between tend windows | Permit quality ceiling for normal treatment | No claimed exact survival forecast; no automated penoxycyline policy or drug treatment |
| Doctor resources | Live medicine stacks; actual skill and nonzero doctor priority; active care/disease excludes staff availability conservatively | Changes permission only | Stack count includes forbidden/unreachable medicines; bed cleanliness, medicine potency and actual tend quality are not guaranteed here |
| Mood and hidden causes | Every instantiated need, all currently evaluated mood thoughts/memories with offsets, actual minor break threshold, ideology precepts | Recreation hour alongside existing recreation/building actions | Thoughts are per-instance offsets, not a claimed sum of final grouped mood impact; no forced social interactions/instant morale |
| Ideological needs and taboos | Actual pawn ideology, certainty, precepts and need definitions; existing director ideology building context; existing augmentation ideology checks | Prisoner conversion when different enabled warden exists | Medical drug approval is exposed as beliefs, not asserted as a full legal validator; rituals, role assignment, conversion abilities, apparel/food/drug policy planning remain incomplete |
| Social relations, romance, grief | Existing pawn relations/opinions endpoints; mood thoughts expose active consequences | No relationship mutation | No autonomous marriage/romance/breakup/relationship repair; relations are not invented |
| Prisoner recruitment | Existing director prisoner handling and `colony_growth.py` route context; resistance/certainty/goal now explicit | Maintain/recruit/reduce resistance/convert policies | No guaranteed recruit rate or automatic goal progression; child prisoner policy, enslavement, release and special Anomaly/Biotech nonexclusive policies are outside this addition |
| Other population routes | `colony_growth.py`: live traders, quests, letters, neutral rescue, wild people; DLC routes explained when active | Existing trader/quest/rescue actions only | Refugee stay, child birth, ritual recruits and creepjoiners are not fabricated as immediately feasible recruits |
| Children and babies (Biotech) | Instantiated Learning/Play needs and current learning desires, health and timetable | One child Work hour to Joy | No school/facility/teacher job placement, baby play/feed automation or growth-moment selection added |
| Roles and work education | `colony_professions.py`: actual WorkTypeDefs, skills/passions and work directions; staff priorities shown here | No role assignment | Ideology specialists, royal titles/permits, mechanitor control and schooling require separate capabilities |
| Gene/DLC-specific needs | All actually instantiated NeedDefs, including modded ones; hediffs and mood causes | No special-system job here | Hemogen, deathrest, psychic entropy, entity containment, dryads and mech work need separate executors |

Thresholds `.9 Learning` and `.5 Joy` are **candidate generation** limits, not asserted native urgent thresholds or forced recommendations. Laya may defer. Staff availability is conservative: enabled work, nonzero priority, controlled colonist, no protected activity and no currently observed medical care need. It does not certify path/reservations; ordinary game jobs retain those checks.

## Primary implementation evidence

Installed `Data/Biotech/Defs/NeedDefs/Needs.xml`: Learning description explicitly limits learning to Recreation/Anything and links satisfied learning to growth tiers; Play is a baby's adult interaction need; Deathrest describes exhaustion and capacity loss. `Data/Core/Defs/Misc/PrisonerInteractionModeDefs/PrisonerInteractionMode.xml`: AttemptRecruit reduces resistance first; ReduceResistance does not attempt recruitment; wild-person and recruitability restrictions. `Data/Ideology/Defs/PrisonerInteractionModeDefs/PrisonerInteractionMode.xml`: Convert follows a warden's ideoligion and is unavailable in classic mode.

Decompiled installed Assembly-CSharp: `RimWorld.Pawn_PlayerSettings.medCare`, `RimWorld.MedicalCareCategory`, `RimWorld.Pawn_GuestTracker.SetExclusiveInteraction` (does not clear nonexclusive modes), `RimWorld.Pawn_TimetableTracker.SetAssignment`, `RimWorld.Need_Learning`, `RimWorld.ThoughtHandler.GetAllMoodThoughts`, `RimWorld.Thought.MoodOffset`, `Verse.AI.MentalBreaker.BreakThresholdMinor`, `RimWorld.Pawn_IdeoTracker.Certainty`, `Verse.Hediff.TendableNow`, `HediffComp_Immunizable.Immunity`.

Supplementary browsed references: [Prisoner](https://www.rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Prisoner), [Children](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Children), [Doctoring](https://www.rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Doctoring). Technical behavior above is based on the installed primary sources.

## Verification

`tests/test_society.py`: ten behavior regressions cover protected care jobs, learning versus sleep preservation, live medicine ceilings, exported prisoner options, both defer stages, stale choice refusal, exact policy posts, applied/deferred cooldown, unrelated director-detail filtering and selected patient diagnosis/stock/beliefs in detailed Laya context. No game launch or live colony mutation was used. C# build is the integration gate performed by the parent agent; gameplay behavior is not runtime-tested.
